Mimic dice chat

Shared chat and dice log for Owlbear Rodeo

Owlbear Rodeo → Extensions → Add extension URL

https://mimic-chat.friendlymimic.com/manifest.json

Why it exists

Roll20 and Foundry have a built-in chat panel that extensions publish dice rolls and events to. Owlbear Rodeo has no equivalent.

Mimic dice chat fills that gap: primarily a dice log, secondarily a chat. Your dice rolls appear here automatically.

  • Dice rolls from Owl20, Owlbear Rodeo Dice, Bones and more
  • Room chat with Markdown and images
  • Inline dice: [1d20+4] or just 2d6
  • Open API for other extensions
